## The Notion Trap

Notion is incredibly powerful. It's a blank canvas that can become anything. But that flexibility is also its biggest weakness.

### 1. Customizing feels productive (but isn't)
Moving database columns, designing the perfect dashboard, and linking properties gives you a dopamine hit of "progress" without actually moving any of your goals forward. It's **meta-productivity** — being productive about productivity instead of being productive.

### 2. Maintenance is endless
The system you build inevitably needs updates. A new template comes out. You discover a better workflow. Your linked database breaks. Soon, maintaining Notion becomes a part-time job.

### 3. No built-in behavior change mechanics
Notion is a **document tool**, not a **behavior change tool**. It doesn't nudge you. It doesn't gamify your habits. It doesn't adapt when you're struggling. It just sits there, waiting for you to update it manually.

### 4. AI is an add-on, not a feature
Notion's AI is great for writing summaries. But it doesn't **coach you**. It doesn't analyze your patterns. It doesn't tell you *what to work on today* based on your actual behavior.
