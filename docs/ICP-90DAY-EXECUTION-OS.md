# RESURGO 90-Day Execution OS (Locked ICP)

## ICP Lock (Do Not Change for 90 Days)
- Primary ICP: indie founders / solo builders shipping a SaaS.
- Core promise: "Turn founder chaos into a shippable weekly plan in under 10 minutes."
- Success metric: weekly shipped output per activated user.

## Weekly Operating Loop
1. Publish one deep BOFU or MOFU asset.
2. Repurpose into X thread, LinkedIn post, Reddit value post, and one email.
3. Capture objections from comments/replies/support chats.
4. Update homepage, pricing, and comparison copy with objection-driven proof.
5. Review funnel metrics and retention metrics every Friday.

## BOFU Moat Priority
1. Competitor alternatives (`/compare/*`) with hard decision framing.
2. "Best X for Y" pages for founder workflows.
3. Pricing-intent reinforcement (outcome + ROI + trust proof).

## Retention Loop Priority
1. First meaningful win in under 10 minutes.
2. Weekly review ritual as a default behavior.
3. Referral ask after clear milestone moments.

## KPI Guardrails (Weekly)
- Visitor -> signup: target 3-5%
- Signup -> activation: target 35-50%
- Activation -> paid: target 4-8%
- Organic share of signups: target 30%+ by day 90

## Non-Negotiables
- No new ICP experiments for 90 days.
- No net-new channels unless one existing channel is stable and repeatable.
- No "vanity launch" tasks that do not improve conversion or retention.
