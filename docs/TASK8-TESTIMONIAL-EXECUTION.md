## TESTIMONIAL RESPONSE FORMATS (Expected Answers)

### Category 1: Shipping/Execution Stories
**Pattern:** "I shipped X in Y time"
- "Shipped 2 features in planning time"
- "Finally launched my side project"
- "Built the thing I'd been procrastinating on for 6 months"

### Category 2: Consistency/Streak Stories  
**Pattern:** "First time I maintained X"
- "First 30-day streak I've ever maintained"
- "First time hitting 90-day consistency"
- "Actually built the habit this time instead of quitting by day 10"

### Category 3: System Replacement Stories
**Pattern:** "Replaced X apps with one"
- "Stopped using Notion for goals, Todoist for tasks, Apple Health for sleep"
- "This is my new operating system"
- "Finally have one dashboard for everything"

### Category 4: ADHD-Specific Stories
**Pattern:** "ADHD + one thing that changed"
- "First time not drowning in a to-do list"
- "One task. Do it. Dopamine hit. That's it."
- "No judgment design helped me actually come back"

### Category 5: Quantified Impact
**Pattern:** "Specific numbers"
- "94-day streak (personal record)"
- "Went from 2% goal completion to 47%"
- "Saved 5 hours/week replacing other apps"
